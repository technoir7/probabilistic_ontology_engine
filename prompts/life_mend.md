# Life mend — v1

Each sentence below was written for an imagined artist from claims critics
made about real artists, listed under it. Some are not whole sentences: an
aside about the artist with nothing after it, or no main verb. For each
sentence, answer "same" if it is a whole sentence. Otherwise mend it into a
whole sentence, only by:

- rearranging its words;
- changing a verb's tense or number;
- adding "is", "was", "has" or "and" where needed;
- joining on one claim from "Claims no sentence uses", in the critic's own
  words.

Use no other word. Keep "the artist" or "they", and keep every hedge
("might", "perhaps", "seems"). End the sentence with the numbers of every
claim it uses, in square brackets, before the full stop.

Answer with one line for each sentence, in order: its number, a space, and
"same" or the mended sentence. Write nothing else. For two sentences, the
first whole and the second mended:
1 same
2 <the mended sentence> [2, 5].
