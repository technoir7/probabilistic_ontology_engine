# Life edit — v1

Below is a short fictional life of an imagined artist, made from claims
critics made about different real artists, joined on purpose. Each sentence
is beside the claims it uses. Some sentences were cut because they changed a
critic's word. Edit the life so it reads well, as a few whole sentences:

- join short sentences, up to three claims in a sentence, with "and", commas
  or an aside about the artist, then go on to what the artist did or is;
- where a sentence was cut, write it again with the critic's own words in
  place of the words listed;
- join claims no sentence uses where they fit;
- keep every claim the life as checked uses.

Use only the words of the claims, "the artist", "they", "them", "their", and
small words that join ("and", "is", "was", "who", "whose", "with", "of").
No word for when, why, after, before, because or but unless a claim has it.
Keep every hedge ("might", "perhaps", "seems"). Keep other people's names as
the claims have them. End each sentence with the numbers of the claims it
uses, in square brackets, before its full stop.
